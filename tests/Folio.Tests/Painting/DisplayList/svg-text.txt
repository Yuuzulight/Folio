=== a tspan repositions and restyles its characters; the text continues after it

---
transform 1,0,0,1,0,0
clip 0,0 200x100
glyphs 10,20 16px rgb(0, 0, 0)
glyphs 100,20 16px rgb(255, 0, 0)
glyphs 116,20 16px rgb(0, 0, 0)
pop
pop
