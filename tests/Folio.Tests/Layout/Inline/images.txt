Replaced elements with natural dimensions (CSS 2.2 sections 10.3.2, 10.4 and 10.6.2; HTML srcset density
selection). The test images are data: URLs: a 4x2 PNG (aspect ratio 2) and a 2x2 PNG.

=== an inline image has its natural size and sits on the baseline
<!DOCTYPE html><style>body, div { margin: 0 }</style><div><img src="data:image/png;base64,iVBORw0KGgoAAAANSUhEUgAAAAQAAAACCAYAAAB/qH1jAAAAEklEQVR4nGP4z8DwH4SRIKoAAAslD/HAvA0nAAAAAElFTkSuQmCC"></div>
---
html 0,0 800x16
  body 0,0 800x16
    div 0,0 800x16
      line 0,0 800x16
        img 0,11 4x2
