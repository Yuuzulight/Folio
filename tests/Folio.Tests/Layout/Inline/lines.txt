=== a short paragraph is one line split at its break opportunity
<!DOCTYPE html><style>body, p { margin: 0 }</style><p>Hello world</p>
---
html 0,0 800x16
  body 0,0 800x16
    p 0,0 800x16
      line 0,0 800x16
        text "Hello " 0,0 96x16
        text "world" 96,0 80x16
