=== border images have their initial values
display: block
---
border-image-source: none
border-image-slice: 100% 100% 100% 100%
border-image-width: 1 1 1 1
border-image-outset: 0 0 0 0
border-image-repeat: stretch

=== slices take numbers and percentages like margins, and fill anywhere
border-image-slice: fill 30 10%
---
border-image-slice: 30 10% 30 10% fill

=== widths take numbers, length-percentages and auto; a unitless zero is a number
border-image-width: 2 1em 25% auto; border-image-outset: 0 1px 0.5
---
border-image-width: 2 16px 25% auto
border-image-outset: 0 1px 0.5 1px

=== repeat takes one or two keywords
border-image-repeat: round space
---
border-image-repeat: round space

=== the shorthand with width and outset
border-image: linear-gradient(red, blue) 27 fill / 1 2px / 3 round
---
border-image-source: linear-gradient(red, blue)
border-image-slice: 27 27 27 27 fill
border-image-width: 1 2px 1 2px
border-image-outset: 3 3 3 3
border-image-repeat: round

=== the shorthand with an outset and no width
border-image: repeat 10% / / 4px linear-gradient(red, blue)
---
border-image-slice: 10% 10% 10% 10%
border-image-width: 1 1 1 1
border-image-outset: 4px 4px 4px 4px
border-image-repeat: repeat

=== the shorthand resets what it leaves out
border-image-width: 3; border-image-repeat: space; border-image: none
---
border-image-source: none
border-image-width: 1 1 1 1
border-image-repeat: stretch

=== the border shorthand resets border images
border-image: linear-gradient(red, blue) 10; border: 2px solid
---
border-image-source: none
border-image-slice: 100% 100% 100% 100%

=== border-top leaves border images alone
border-image-slice: 10; border-top: 2px solid
---
border-image-slice: 10 10 10 10

=== invalid border images are dropped
border-image-slice: 5; border-image-slice: -1; border-image-slice: 1px; border-image-slice: fill; border-image-slice: 1 2 3 4 5; border-image-width: -1; border-image-outset: 10%; border-image-outset: auto; border-image-repeat: stretch stretch stretch; border-image: 10 / ; border-image: red
---
border-image-slice: 5 5 5 5
border-image-width: 1 1 1 1
border-image-outset: 0 0 0 0
border-image-repeat: stretch
