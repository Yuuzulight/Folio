=== absolute units
width: 1in; height: 2.54cm; min-width: 12pt; min-height: 1pc; max-width: 10mm; max-height: 4q
---
width: 96px
height: 96px
min-width: 16px
min-height: 16px
max-width: 37.795px
max-height: 3.78px

=== font-relative units use the element's font size
font-size: 20px; width: 2em; height: 1rem; margin-top: 2ex; margin-left: 1ch
---
width: 40px
height: 16px
margin-top: 20px
margin-left: 10px

=== em in font-size refers to the parent
%parent font-size: 20px
font-size: 1.5em; width: 1em
---
font-size: 30
width: 30px

=== viewport units
width: 50vw; height: 10vh; min-width: 10vmin; min-height: 10vmax; max-height: 100dvh
---
width: 400px
height: 60px
min-width: 60px
min-height: 80px
max-height: 600px

=== percentages stay percentages
width: 50%; padding-left: 10%; margin-top: -5%
---
width: 50%
padding-left: 10%
margin-top: -5%

=== calc with lengths and percentages stays linear
width: calc(100% - 2em); height: calc((10px + 5%) * 2); margin-left: calc(3 * 10px / 2)
---
width: -32px + 100%
height: 20px + 10%
margin-left: 15px

=== min, max and clamp fold unless they hold a percentage
width: min(10px, 2em); height: clamp(10px, 50px, 30px); min-width: max(10px, 20%)
---
width: 10px
height: 30px
min-width: calc(...)

=== invalid calc leaves the initial value
width: calc(10px + 2); height: calc(10px*2px); margin-top: calc(1px -2px)
---
width: auto
height: auto
margin-top: 0px

=== negative sizes and paddings are invalid
width: -10px; padding-top: -1px
---
width: auto
padding-top: 0px

=== unitless zero is a length, other unitless numbers are not
margin-top: 0; margin-left: 10
---
margin-top: 0px
margin-left: 0px

=== sizing keywords
width: min-content; height: fit-content; max-width: none; min-height: auto
---
width: min-content
height: fit-content
max-width: none
min-height: auto
