=== paragraphs close each other
<!DOCTYPE html><p>a<p>b<div>c</div>
---
#document
  <!DOCTYPE html>
  <html>
    <head>
    <body>
      <p>
        "a"
      <p>
        "b"
      <div>
        "c"
