=== svg with adjusted names and an HTML integration point
<!DOCTYPE html><svg viewbox="0 0 10 10"><lineargradient id=g></lineargradient><foreignobject><p>x</p></foreignobject></svg>
---
#document
  <!DOCTYPE html>
  <html>
    <head>
    <body>
      <svg:svg viewBox="0 0 10 10">
        <svg:linearGradient id="g">
        <svg:foreignObject>
          <p>
            "x"
