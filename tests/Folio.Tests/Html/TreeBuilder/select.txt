=== options close each other
<!DOCTYPE html><select><option>a<option>b<optgroup><option>c</select>x
---
#document
  <!DOCTYPE html>
  <html>
    <head>
    <body>
      <select>
        <option>
          "a"
        <option>
          "b"
        <optgroup>
          <option>
            "c"
      "x"
