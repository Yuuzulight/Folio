---

---

---

=== nth selectors

---
:nth-child(2n+1) (0,1,0)

=== logical pseudo-classes take their most specific argument
:is(.a, #b) p, :where(#c) p, :not(.x, div)
---
:is(.a, #b) p (1,0,1)
:where(#c) p (0,0,1)
:not(.x, div) (0,1,0)

=== is and where forgive invalid arguments
:is(.a, :bogus), :where(:bogus)
---
:is(.a) (0,1,0)
:where() (0,0,0)

=== not does not forgive
:not(.a, :bogus)
---
invalid

=== one invalid selector invalidates the list
.a, .b:bogus
---
invalid

=== has arrives later
:has(.a)
---
invalid

=== nothing follows a pseudo-element

---
invalid

invalid

---
