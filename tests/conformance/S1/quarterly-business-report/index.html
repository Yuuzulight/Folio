Overview

Larkspur closed the third quarter with revenue of $48.2 million, up 14% on the same quarter last year and 3% ahead of plan. Growth came almost entirely from our direct channels: the online store and our nine retail locations together grew 30%, while wholesale revenue fell 6% as two large outdoor chains reduced their autumn orders.1

Gross margin improved to 52.8%, helped by the mix shift toward direct sales and by lower freight costs. Operating expenses rose faster than revenue, mostly because of the new Portland store and a heavier marketing push for the Ridgeline tent launch, but operating income still grew by 9%.

Direct to consumer

The online store had its best quarter ever. Conversion rose from 2.6% to 3.1% after the checkout redesign in June, and the average order value held steady at $118. Repeat customers now account for 44% of online revenue, up from 37%.

For the first time, more than half of our revenue came from customers who buy directly from us. — Maya Castellanos, Chief Executive

Wholesale

Wholesale remains our largest single channel, but it is under pressure. Two national retailers cut their autumn orders by a combined $2.3 million, citing high inventory. Independent specialty shops, by contrast, grew 8%. We expect wholesale to be flat to slightly down in Q4.

Products

The Ridgeline two-person tent sold 14,200 units in its first ten weeks, beating its launch forecast by 40%. Apparel was flat, and the footwear line continued to underperform; we are reviewing whether to keep it beyond spring.2

Outlook for Q4

  • We are raising full-year revenue guidance from $176M to $181M.
  • Holiday marketing spend will be 12% higher than last year, weighted toward email and returning customers.
  • The Denver store opens on 14 November, bringing the fleet to ten.
  • Wholesale guidance is lowered to $64M for the year, from $68M.
