Lisbon & Porto

Six days for two, 12–17 May. Trams, tiles, custard tarts and a slow train north.

6 days3 nights Lisbon, 2 nights Porto
€1,860estimated total for two
Mild18–23 °C, low rain chance

Day by day

DAY 1

Arrive and wander Alfama

Tue 12 May
  1. 11:40 Land at Lisbon airport; metro red line to Alameda, then green line to Baixa-Chiado (about 35 min).
  2. 14:00 Check in at Casa do Largo, Alfama.
  3. 15:30 Walk up to the Miradouro de Santa Luzia.
    • Continue to the castle if energy allows (€15 each).
    • Otherwise, coffee on the terrace at Portas do Sol.
  4. 20:00 Dinner and live fado at a small tasca. Reservation under "Morgan".
DAY 2

Belém by tram

Wed 13 May
  1. 09:00 Tram 15 from Praça da Figueira to Belém.
  2. 09:45 Jerónimos Monastery (timed ticket, 10:00 entry).
  3. 12:00 Custard tarts at the famous bakery, eaten warm with cinnamon.
  4. 14:00 Riverside walk to the Monument to the Discoveries and the tower.
  5. 19:30 Sunset drinks in Bairro Alto.

Heads up: the monastery is closed on Mondays, which is why Belém is on a Wednesday.

DAY 3

Day trip to Sintra

Thu 14 May
  1. 08:30 Train from Rossio station (40 min, €2.40 each way).
  2. 09:30 Bus 434 up the hill.
    1. Pena Palace first, before the crowds.
    2. Walk down through the park to the Moorish Castle.
    3. Lunch in the old town.
  3. 15:00 Quinta da Regaleira and its initiation well.
  4. 18:30 Train back to Lisbon; early night.
DAY 4

North to Porto

Fri 15 May
  1. 09:39 Fast train from Santa Apolónia to Porto Campanhã (2 h 50 min). Sit on the left for river views near Coimbra.
  2. 13:30 Check in at Rua das Flores Guesthouse.
  3. 15:00 São Bento station to see the blue-and-white tile panels, then down to the Ribeira waterfront.
  4. 20:00 Dinner: grilled sardines and vinho verde.
DAY 5

Port lodges and the Douro

Sat 16 May
  1. 10:00 Walk across the upper deck of the Dom Luís I bridge to Vila Nova de Gaia.
  2. 11:00 Cellar tour and tasting (booked, 11:00 English tour).
  3. 14:30 Six-bridges river cruise, 50 minutes.
  4. 17:00 Livraria Lello, if the queue is under 20 minutes.
DAY 6

Foz and fly home

Sun 17 May
  1. 09:30 Tram 1 along the river to Foz do Douro for a seaside breakfast.
  2. 13:00 Collect bags; metro violet line to the airport (35 min).
  3. 16:25 Flight home.

Allow extra time on Sunday: the metro runs every 20 minutes instead of 10.
