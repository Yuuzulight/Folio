Roasted Squash and Sage Galette

A rustic, free-form tart for chilly autumn evenings.

6servings
30 minprep
45 minbake
200 °Coven

Ingredients

Pastry

  • 190 g plain flour
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • 115 g cold butter, cubed
  • 4–5 tbsp ice water

Filling

  • 1 small butternut squash (about 700 g)
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 10 fresh sage leaves
  • 80 g soft goat's cheese
  • ¼ tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Method

Make the pastry

  1. Whisk the flour and salt, then rub in the butter until it looks like coarse crumbs with a few pea-sized lumps.
  2. Add the water a spoonful at a time until the dough just holds together.
  3. Flatten into a disc, wrap, and chill for at least 30 minutes.

Fill and bake

  1. Peel and slice the squash into 5 mm half-moons. Toss with the onion, oil and chilli.
  2. Roll the pastry into a 30 cm circle on baking paper.
  3. Crumble half the cheese over the centre, leaving a 5 cm border. Layer the squash on top.
  4. Fold the border over the filling, pleating as you go. Brush the pastry with egg.
  5. Bake for 40–45 minutes until the pastry is deep golden and the squash is tender.
  6. Fry the sage in a little oil until crisp, then scatter over with the remaining cheese.
Make ahead: the pastry keeps for 3 days in the fridge or a month in the freezer. Thaw overnight before rolling.
