How we handle refund requests

Support playbook · Updated 14 September 2026 · Owner: Customer Care

Every refund request follows the same path, whether it arrives by email, chat or phone. The flowchart below is the source of truth; the numbered notes explain the judgement calls.

  1. Order lookup. If we can't find an order within five minutes, ask the customer for a receipt photo before going further.
  2. Store credit. Outside the 30-day window we offer store credit worth 110% of the purchase price. Most customers accept.
  3. Returns. Digital goods never need a return; skip straight to the refund once the licence is revoked.
