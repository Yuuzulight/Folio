Does more study time pay off?

A. Mensah and L. Fischer · Statistics 201 project · Fall 2026

1. Method

We surveyed 40 students in two sections of Introductory Statistics about the hours they studied during the week before the midterm, then matched the answers to their midterm scores. Students in the morning section are shown in blue and the evening section in orange.

2. Results

SectionnMean hoursMean scorer
Morning206.176.80.74
Evening205.873.20.68
All405.9575.00.71

The relationship is strong and roughly linear in both sections. Evening students scored slightly lower at every level of study time, which may reflect that many of them work during the day.
