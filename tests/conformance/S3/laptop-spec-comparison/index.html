Our pick: Corvid Aero 14

The Aero 14 is not the fastest or the cheapest laptop here, but it gets the balance right: a bright OLED panel, over 16 hours of battery, a full-size HDMI port and a 1 TB drive at a price well under the Brightline.

If you need a card reader or more than 16 GB of memory, the Norrland Studio 15 is the better buy for $250 more.
